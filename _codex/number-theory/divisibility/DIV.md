> {% codex DIV#DEF:Divisor %}
>
> Let $n$, $m \in \N$. $n$ **divides** $m$, written
>
> $$ n \divides m, $$
>
> if there exists $k \in \N$ such that
>
> $$ m = kn. $$

- "$n$ divides $m$" is also phrased as
    - $n$ is a **divisor** or **factor** of $m$;
    - $m$ is a **multiple** of $n$;
    - $m$ is **divisible** by $n$.

---

> {% codex DIV#PROP-ZERO %}
>
> {:.par-upper-roman}
> 1. For all $n \in \N$,
>
>    $$ 0 \divides n. $$
>
> 2. For all $n \in \N^+$,
>
>    $$ n \ndivides 0. $$
