{% entry DEF-1 %}

Let $n$, $m \in \N$. $n$ **divides** $m$, written

$$ n \divides m, $$

if there exists $k \in \N$ such that

$$ m = kn. $$

{% endentry %}

***Note.*** "$n$ divides $m$" is also phrased as:

- $n$ is a **divisor** or **factor** of $m$;
- $m$ is a **multiple** of $n$;
- $m$ is **divisible** by $n$.

{% entry PROP-1 %}

{:.par-upper-roman}
1. For all $n \in \N$,

   $$ 0 \divides n. $$

2. For all $n \in \N^+$,

   $$ n \ndivides 0. $$

{% endentry %}
