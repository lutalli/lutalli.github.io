> {% codex BALL#DEF:Ball %}
>
> Let $X$ be a metric space, $a \in X$ and $r \in \R^+$. The **open ball** of radius $r$ around $a$ is
>
> $$ \ball_{< r}(a) = \{x \in X : d(a, x) < r\}. $$
>
> The **closed ball** of radius $r$ around $a$ is
>
> $$ \ball_{\leq r}(a) = \{x \in X : d(a, x) \leq r\}. $$
