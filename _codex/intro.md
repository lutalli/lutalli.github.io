**Codex** is my personal math logbook. It's mostly made of *axioms*, *definitions* and *propositions*. Here, *proposition* is a generalized term for mathematical results, such as theorems, lemmas, etc.

Every page in Codex is assigned with a unique **slug**, which consists of up to 8 uppercase letters. For example, the slug of [Disjoint Union](/codex/DJU) is `DJU`.

We work in $\ZF$ by default.
