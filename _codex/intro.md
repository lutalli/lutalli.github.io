**Codex** is my personal online math notebook. It's mostly made of *definitions*, *axioms* and *theorems*. Here, *theorem* is a generalized term for mathematical results (there is no *propositions*, *lemmas*, etc.).

Every page in Codex is assigned with a unique **slug**, which consists of up to 8 uppercase letters. For example, the slug of [Disjoint Union](/codex/DJU) is DJU.

We work in $\ZF$ by default.
