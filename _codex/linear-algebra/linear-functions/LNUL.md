{% entry DEF-1 %}

Let $V$, $W$ be vector spaces over a field $F$ and $f : V \to W$ be a linear mapping. By [LF > Proposition 8 (I)](/codex/LF#PROP-8), $\ker f$ is a subspace. The **nullity** of $f$ is the dimension of $\ker f$:

$$ \null f = \dim \ker f. $$
