{% entry DEF-1 %}

Let $V$, $W$ be vector spaces over a field $F$ and $f : V \to W$ be a linear function. By {% entry_ref LF#PROP-4 %}, $\im f$ is a subspace. The **rank** of $f$ is the dimension of $\im f$:

$$ \rank f = \dim \im f. $$
