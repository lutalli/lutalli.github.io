> {{ "VSUM#DEF:Sum of Vector Spaces" | cod }}
>
> Let $V$ be a [vector space](/codex/VS) and $X$, $Y\subseteq V$ be [subspaces](/codex/SVS). The **sum** of $X$ and $Y$ is
>
> $$ X + Y = \{x+y : x\in X, y\in Y\}. $$

---

> {{ "VSUM#PROP-S" | cod }}
>
> Let $V$ be a vector space. For any subspaces $X$, $Y\subseteq V$:
>
> {:.par-upper-roman}
> 1. $X+Y$ is a subspace.
> 2. $X+Y = \langle X\cup Y\rangle$.

> {{ "VSUM#PROP-DIM" | cod }}
>
> Let $V$ be a vector space. For any [finite-dimensional](/codex/DIM) subspaces $X$, $Y\subseteq V$,
>
> $$ \dim(X+Y) = \dim X + \dim Y - \dim(X\cap Y). $$

As a corollary:

> {{ "VSUM#PROP-DIM-I" | cod }}
>
> Let $V$ be a vector space. For any finite-dimensional subspaces $X$, $Y\subseteq V$,
>
> $$ \dim(X+Y) \leq \dim X + \dim Y. $$

---

## Notation

$\dim V$
: The [dimension](/codex/DIM) of $V$.
