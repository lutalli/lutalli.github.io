> {{ "DIM#DEF:Dimension" | cod }} <span class="adjunctive">$\limp\AC$</span>
>
> Let $V$ be a [vector space](/codex/VS). By [BS#PROP-EX](/codex/BS#PROP-EX), $V$ has a [basis](/codex/BS). By [BS#PROP-L](/codex/BS#PROP-L), all bases of $V$ have the same [cardinality](/codex/CARD). This cardinality is called the **dimension** of $V$, written as $\dim V$.
>
> $V$ is **finite-dimensional** if $\dim V$ is finite; **infinite-dimensional** if $\dim V$ is infinite.

---

> {{ "DIM#PROP-S" | cod }}
>
> Let $V$ be a vector space. For any [subspace](/codex/SVS) $W\subseteq V$:
>
> {:.par-upper-roman}
> 1. $\dim W \leq \dim V$.
> 2. $\dim W = \dim V \enspace\rimp\enspace W = V$.
