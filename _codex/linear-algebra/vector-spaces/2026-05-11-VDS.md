> {{ "VDS#DEF:Direct Sum" | cod }}
>
> Let $V$ be a [vector space](/codex/VS) and $X$, $Y\subseteq V$ be [subspaces](/codex/SVS).  The [sum](/codex/VSUM) $X+Y$ is **direct** if $X\cap Y=\\{0\\}$. The notation
>
> $$ W = X \oplus Y $$
>
> means
>
> $$ W = X + Y \enspace\land\enspace X\cap Y = \{0\}. $$
>
> If $V=X\oplus Y$, $X$ and $Y$ are called **complementary subspaces** to each other.

---

> {{ "VDS#PROP-A" | cod }}
>
> Let $V$ be a vector space and $X$, $Y\subseteq V$ be subspaces such that $V=X+Y$. The following statements are equivalent:
>
> {:.par-upper-roman}
> 1. $V = X\oplus Y$.
> 2. For each $v\in V$ there are unique $x\in X$ and $y\in Y$ such that $v=x+y$.
> 3. For any $x\in X$ and $y\in Y$, if $x\neq 0$ and $y\neq 0$, then $x$ and $y$ are [linear independent](/codex/LI).

> {{ "VDS#PROP-B" | cod }}
>
> Let $V$ be a vector space and $X$, $Y\subseteq V$ be [finite-dimensional](/codex/DIM) subspaces. The following statements are equivalent:
>
> {:.par-upper-roman}
> 1. $V = X \oplus Y$.
> 2. $X$ has a [basis](/codex/BS) $\mathcal{A}$ and $Y$ has a basis $\mathcal{B}$ such that $\mathcal{A}\cup\mathcal{B}$ is a basis of $V$.
> 3. $V = X + Y$ and $\dim V = \dim X + \dim Y$.

## Notation

$\dim V$
: The [dimension](/codex/DIM) of $V$.
