> {{ "LNLT#DEF:Nullity" | cod }}
>
> Let $V$, $W$ be [vector spaces](/codex/VS) over a [field](/codex/FLD) $F$ and $f : V \to W$ be a [linear mapping](/codex/LM). By [LM#PROP-K (I)](/codex/LM#PROP-K), $\ker f$ is a [subspace](/codex/SVS). The **nullity** of $f$ is the [dimension](/codex/DIM) of $\ker f$:
>
> $$ \null f = \dim \ker f. $$
