> {% codex LNLT#DEF:Nullity %}
>
> Let $V$, $W$ be vector spaces over a field $F$ and $f : V \to W$ be a linear mapping. By [LM#PROP-K (I)](/codex/LM#PROP-K), $\ker f$ is a subspace. The **nullity** of $f$ is the dimension of $\ker f$:
>
> $$ \null f = \dim \ker f. $$
