> {% codex LRK#DEF:Rank %}
>
> Let $V$, $W$ be vector spaces over a field $F$ and $f : V \to W$ be a linear mapping. By [LM#PROP-K (I)](/codex/LM#PROP-K), $\im f$ is a subspace. The **rank** of $f$ is the dimension of $\im f$:
>
> $$ \rank f = \dim \im f. $$
