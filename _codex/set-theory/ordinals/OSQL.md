{% entry DEF-1 %}

Let $\alpha$ be a [limit ordinal](/codex/LIMO) and $\gamma$ be an [increasing](/codex/OH) $\alpha$-sequence of [ordinals](/codex/ORD). The **limit** of $\gamma$ is

$$ \lim_{\xi \to \alpha} \gamma_\xi = \sup \{\gamma_\xi : \xi < \alpha\}. $$
