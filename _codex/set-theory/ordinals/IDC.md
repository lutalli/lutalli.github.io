> {% codex IDC#PROP:Induction %}
>
> Let $A$ be a subset of $\N$. Suppose:
>
> {:.par-upper-roman}
> 1. $$ 0 \in A. $$
>
> 2. $$ \forall n \in A : n + 1 \in A. $$
>
> Then $A = \N$.

***Proof.*** Otherwise, $\min(\N \smallsetminus A)$ would be a limit ordinal, contrary to [N#PROP-LO](/codex/N#PROP-LO).<qed />
