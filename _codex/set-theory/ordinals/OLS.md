> {% codex OLS#DEF:Limit of Ordinal Sequence %}
>
> Let $\alpha$ be a limit ordinal and $\gamma$ be an increasing $\alpha$-sequence of ordinals. The **limit** of $\gamma$ is
>
> $$ \lim_{\xi \to \alpha} \gamma_\xi = \sup \{\gamma_\xi : \xi < \alpha\}. $$
