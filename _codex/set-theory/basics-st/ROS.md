> {% codex ROS#DEF:Roster Notation %}
>
> For any sets $a_1$, $\cdots$, $a_n$, ($n \geq 3$) we write
>
> $$ \{a_1, \cdots, a_n\} = \{a_1\} \cup \cdots \cup \{a_n\}. $$

- *Roster Notation* is also known as *Enumeration Notation*.
