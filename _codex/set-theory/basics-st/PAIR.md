> {% codex PAIR#DEF:Pair %}
>
> Let $a$ and $b$ be two sets. The **pair** of $a$ and $b$ is
>
> $$ \{a, b\} = \{x \,:\, x = a \,\lor\, x = b\}. $$
>
> This is a set by [Axiom of Pairing](/codex/ZF#AX-PAIR).

---

> {% codex PAIR#PROP-UO:Unorderedness %}
>
> For any sets $a$ and $b$,
>
> $$ \{a, b\} = \{b, a\}. $$
