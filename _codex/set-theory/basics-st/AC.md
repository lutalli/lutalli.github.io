---
title:   Axiom of Choice
date:    2026-03-06
updated: 2026-06-26
---

{% entry DEF-1 %}

Let $X$ be a non-empty set. A **choice function** on $X$ is a function on $X$ such that

$$ \forall A \in X : f(A) \in A. $$

{% endentry %}

{% entry AX-1:Axiom of Choice ($\AC$) %}

Every set has a choice function.

{% endentry %}

---

{% entry PROP-1 %}

$\AC$ is independent of $\ZF$.

{% endentry %}

***Note.*** $\ZF + \AC$ is denoted by $\ZFC$.
