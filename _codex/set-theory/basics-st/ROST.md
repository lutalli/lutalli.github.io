---
title:   Roster Notation
date:    2026-02-16
updated: 2026-06-23
---

{% entry DEF-1 %}

For any sets $a_1$, $\cdots$, $a_n$ ($n \geq 3$), we write

$$ \{a_1, \cdots, a_n\} = \{a_1\} \cup \cdots \cup \{a_n\}. $$

{% endentry %}

***Note.*** *Roster Notation* is also known as *Enumeration Notation*.
