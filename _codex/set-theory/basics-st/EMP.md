> {% codex EMP#DEF:Empty Set %}
>
> The **empty set** $\empt$ is the unique set such that
>
> $$ \forall x : x \notin \empt. $$
>
> The existence of $\empt$ is justified by [Axiom of Empty Set](/codex/ZF#AX-EMP), [Axiom of Existence](/codex/ZF#AX-EX) or [Axiom of Infinity](/codex/ZF#AX-INF). Its uniqueness is justified by [Axiom of Extensionality](/codex/ZF#AX-EXT).
