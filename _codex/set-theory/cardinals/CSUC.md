---
title:   Cardinal Successor
date:    2026-03-09
updated: 2026-06-10
---

{% entry PROP-1 %}

For every ordinal $\alpha$ there is a cardinal greater than $\alpha$.

{% endentry %}

{% entry DEF-1 %}

The **cardinal successor** of an ordinal $\alpha$, denoted by $\alpha^+$, is the least cardinal greater than $\alpha$.

{% endentry %}
