{% definition 1 %}

An **injective function** or **injection** is a [function](/codex/F) $f : X \to Y$ such that

$$ \forall x, y \in X :\enspace f(x) = f(y) \enspace\rimp\enspace x = y. $$

The set of all injections from $X$ to $Y$ is denoted by

$$ \inj(X, Y). $$

{% enddefinition %}

> An *injective function* is also known as a **one-to-one function**.
