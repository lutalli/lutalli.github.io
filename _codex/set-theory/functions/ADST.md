date:    2026-04-18

{% entry DEF-1 %}

Let $f$ be a [function](/codex/F) on a set $X$ and $\*$ be a [binary operation](/codex/F#DEF-2) on $X$. $f$ is **antidistributive** over $\*$ if for all $x$, $y \in X$,

$$ f(x * y) = f(y) * f(x). $$
