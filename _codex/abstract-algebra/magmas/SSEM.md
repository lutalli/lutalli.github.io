> {% codex SSEM#DEF:Subsemigroup %}
>
> Let $(S, \*)$ be a semigroup. $(T, \*)$ is a **subsemigroup** of $(S, \*)$, written $S \leq T$, if $(S, \*)$ itself is a semigroup and $S \subseteq T$.

---

> {% codex SSEM#PROP-TST %}
>
> Let $(S, \*)$ be a semigroup. $T \subseteq S$ is a subsemigroup as soon as $T$ is closed under $\*$, i.e. for all $a$, $b \in T$,
>
> $$ ab \in T. $$
