{% definition 1 %}

A **magma homomorphism** between two [magmas](/codex/MAG) $M$ and $N$ is a [function](/codex/F) $f : M \to N$ such that for all $a$, $b \in M$,

$$ f(a b) = f(a) f(b). $$

{% enddefinition %}
