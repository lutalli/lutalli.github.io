> {% codex KD#DEF:Kronecker Delta %}
>
> For any $i$ and $j$, we write
>
> $$ \delta_{i, j} = \begin{cases}
      1, & \text{if $i = j$}, \\
      0, & \text{if $i \neq j$},
  \end{cases} $$
>
> Using Iverson Brackets we have
>
> $$ \delta_{i, j} = [i = j]. $$
