{% definition 1 %}

A **semigroup** is an [associative](/codex/OPC) [magma](/codex/MAG), i.e. a magma $S$ such that for all $a$, $b$, $c \in S$,

$$ (ab)c = a(bc). $$

{% enddefinition %}
