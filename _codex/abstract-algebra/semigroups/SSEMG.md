{% definition 1 %}

Let $(S, \*)$ be a [semigroup](/codex/SEMG). $(T, \*)$ is a **subsemigroup** of $(S, \*)$, written $S \leq T$, if $(S, \*)$ itself is a semigroup and $S \subseteq T$.

{% enddefinition %}

{% proposition 1 "Subsemigroup Test" %}

Let $(S, \*)$ be a semigroup. $T \subseteq S$ is a subsemigroup as soon as $T$ is closed under $\*$, i.e. for all $a$, $b \in T$,

$$ ab \in T. $$

{% endproposition %}
