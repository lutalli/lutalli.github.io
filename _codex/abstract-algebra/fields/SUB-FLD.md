{% entry DEF-1 %}

Let $(F, +, \cdot)$ be a field. $(K, +, \cdot)$ is a **subfield** of $F$ if it is a field itself and $K \subseteq F$.

{% endentry %}

---

{% entry PROP-1 %}

Let $F$ be a field. $K$ is a subfield of $F$ as soon as:

{:.par-upper-roman}
1. $K$ is a subring.

2. (*Closure under inversion*) For all $a \in K \setdif \\{0\\}$,

   $$ a^{-1} \in K. $$

{% endentry %}
