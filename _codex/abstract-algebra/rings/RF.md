> {% codex RF#DEF:Ring of Functions %}
>
> Let $R$ be a ring and $A$ be a non-empty set. The **ring of functions** from $A$ to $R$ is the ring $\func(A, R)$ with addition and multiplication defined by
>
> $$ \begin{align*}
      f + g &: a\mapsto f(a) + g(a), \\
      fg    &: a\mapsto f(a)g(a)
  \end{align*} $$
>
> for all $f$, $g \in \func(A, R)$. The neutral elements are the constant functions
>
> $$ \underline{0} : a \mapsto 0, \quad \underline{1} : a \mapsto 1. $$
