> {% codex DOM#DEF:Domain %}
>
> A **domain** is a non-zero ring $R$ such that for all $a$, $b \in R$,
>
> $$ ab = 0 \enspace\rimp\enspace a = 0 \,\lor\, b = 0. $$
>
> In other words, a domain is a non-zero ring without zero divisors.
