{% definition CH %}

Let $R$ be a ring. The **characteristic** of $R$, denoted by

$$ \chara R, $$

is the smallest $n \in \N^+$ such that

$$ n \cdot 1 = 0, $$

if it exists. Otherwise, we let $\chara R = 0$.

{% enddefinition %}
