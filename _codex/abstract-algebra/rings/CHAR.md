> {% codex CHAR#DEF:Characteristic %}
>
> Let $R$ be a ring. The **characteristic** of $R$, written as $\chara R$, is the smallest $n \in \N^+$ such that
>
> $$ n \cdot 1 = 0, $$
>
> if it exists; otherwise, we let $\chara R = 0$.
