date:    2026-03-18

{% entry DEF-1 %}

Let $G$ be a group and $S \subseteq G$ be a subset. The **subgroup generated by $S$** is the minimal subgroup that contains $S$:

$$ \langle S \rangle = \bigcap \{H \leq G : S \subseteq H\}. $$

If $H$ is a subgroup and $\langle S \rangle = H$, we say $S$ **generates** $H$. The elements of $S$ are then called **generators**.

{% endentry %}

---

{% entry PROP-1 %}

For any group $G$ with neutral element $e$:

{:.par-upper-roman}
1. $$ \langle \empt \rangle = \{e\}. $$

2. $$ \langle \{e\} \rangle = G. $$

{% endentry %}
