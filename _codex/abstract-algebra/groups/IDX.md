{% definition 1 %}

Let $G$ be a group and $H$ be a subgroup. The **index** of $H$ in $G$ is

$$ \lvert G / H \rvert, $$

the cardinality of the left coset quotient of $H$.

{% enddefinition %}

{% proposition 1 %}

For any three groups $A \leq B \leq C$,

$$ \lvert C / A\rvert = \lvert C / B \rvert \cdot \lvert B / A \rvert. $$

{% endproposition %}
