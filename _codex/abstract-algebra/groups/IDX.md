{% entry DEF-1 %}

Let $G$ be a group and $H$ be a subgroup. The **index** of $H$ in $G$ is

$$ \lvert G / H \rvert, $$

the cardinality of the set of all left cosets of $H$.

{% endentry %}

---

{% entry PROP-1 %}

For any three groups $K \leq H \leq G$,

$$ \lvert G / K\rvert = \lvert G / H \rvert \cdot \lvert H / K \rvert. $$

{% endentry %}
