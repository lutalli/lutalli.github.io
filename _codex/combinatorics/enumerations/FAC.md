> {% codex FAC#DEF:Factorial %}
>
> The **factorial** of $n \in \N$, written $n!$, is defined recursively:
>
> {:.par-upper-roman}
> 1. $$ 0! = 1. $$
>
> 2. For all $n \in \N^+$,
>
>    $$ n! = (n - 1)! \cdot n. $$

- For $n \in \N^+$ we have

  $$ n! = \prod_{k = 1}^n k. $$
