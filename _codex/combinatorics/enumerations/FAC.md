{% entry DEF-1 %}

The **factorial** of $n \in \N$, written $n!$, is defined recursively:

{:.par-upper-roman}
1. $$ 0! = 1. $$

2. For all $n \in \N^+$,

   $$ n! = (n - 1)! \cdot n. $$

{% endentry %}

***Note.*** For $n \in \N^+$ we have

$$ n! = \prod_{k = 1}^n k. $$
