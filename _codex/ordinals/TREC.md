{% theorem 1 "Transfinite Recursion" %}

For any [function](/x/F) $G$ on $\V$, there is a unique function $F$ on $\Ord$ such that for all $\alpha \in \Ord$,

$$ F(\alpha) = G(F \restriction_\alpha). $$

{% endtheorem %}
